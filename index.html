The game

Explain it, don't say it.

WELLIAS is an alias-style word-guessing party game for nutritionists, health coaches and anyone curious about food and wellness. Describe the term on your card without saying it, and your team has to guess before the timer runs out.

Six levels

From everyday foods to nutritionist-level science, so beginners and pros can play together

Three languages

English, Russian and Spanish

Party mode

Pass the phone around, play in teams, or skip the timer and just talk

Frequently asked questions

I bought "Unlock Nutraceuticals" or "Remove Ads" on another device. How do I get it back?Open WELLIAS, tap the stats icon on the level-select screen, and use the "Restore purchase" link in either purchase screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not to a specific phone.
I was charged but the level/feature didn't unlock.First try "Restore purchase" (see above). If that doesn't resolve it, email us with your Apple order ID (found in your Apple ID purchase history) and we'll help sort it out.
How do I request a refund?All payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled directly through Apple, not by us. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Can I change the game's language?Yes — tap the flag icon on the level-select screen to switch between English, Russian, and Spanish at any time.
I found a translation mistake or a term that seems wrong.Thank you — please email us the exact term and level, and we'll take a look.
How do I turn off sound or ads?Sound: tap the speaker icon on the level-select screen. Ads: purchase "Remove Ads" from the stats screen to remove them permanently.
