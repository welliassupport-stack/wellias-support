Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

WELLIAS ("the App", "we", "us") is a word-guessing party game about nutrition and wellness terms. It is developed and operated by Alina Maksurova, an individual developer based in Montenegro, who is the controller of the information described in this Policy.

Questions about this Policy can be sent to: wellias.support@gmail.com

2. The Short Version

WELLIAS has no accounts and no servers of our own that store your gameplay. Your settings and scores stay on your device. The only information that leaves your device goes to the services that make purchases and ads work (Apple, RevenueCat, Google AdMob), and to us if you write to us for support. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The App stores the following on your device only. It is not sent to us:

  • Your selected language (English, Russian, or Spanish)
  • Your sound on/off preference
  • Whether you have made the in-app purchases "Unlock Nutraceuticals" and "Remove Ads"
  • Your personal best scores and rounds played per level
  • Whether you have already seen the onboarding tour

If you delete the App or clear its data, this information is deleted permanently. We cannot recover it because we never had a copy.

4. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services. They collect information on their own behalf and under their own privacy policies.

4.1 Apple. In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

4.2 RevenueCat. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to validate purchases, restore them on your devices, and check whether you own "Unlock Nutraceuticals" or "Remove Ads". RevenueCat may receive an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase and receipt information, device and app version information, and your IP address. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

4.3 Google AdMob. Unless you have bought "Remove Ads", the App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob between rounds. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about app usage and ad interactions, in order to deliver, measure, and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

4.4 Your choices about advertising. On iOS, the App asks for your permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before ads may use the advertising identifier for tracking. If you decline, ads may still be shown but will not be personalized using that identifier. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App shows a consent message (using Google's User Messaging Platform) where you can accept or refuse personalized ads and change your choice later. You can also change tracking permissions at any time in your device settings, or remove ads entirely by purchasing "Remove Ads".

5. Information You Send Us

If you contact us for support, for example at wellias.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use it only to answer you and to resolve your request, and we do not use it for marketing.

6. How and Why We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide the game and keep your purchases working across your devices
  • Process and validate in-app purchases and restore them when you ask
  • Show and measure advertising, where you have not bought "Remove Ads"
  • Respond to support requests
  • Maintain the App, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations

Where data protection law such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, our legal bases are: performance of our contract with you (purchases and restoring them), your consent (personalized advertising and tracking, which you can withdraw at any time), our legitimate interests (keeping the App secure and working, answering your messages), and compliance with legal obligations.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with:

  • Apple, to distribute the App and process purchases
  • RevenueCat, to manage purchases and entitlements
  • Google AdMob, to serve and measure ads
  • Authorities or courts, if the law requires it

These third parties act under their own terms and policies. Depending on where you live, advertising data collection by ad networks may be treated by law as "sharing" or "sale" for advertising purposes. You can opt out as described in Section 4.4.

8. International Transfers

Apple, RevenueCat, and Google operate globally and may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on safeguards offered by those providers, such as standard contractual clauses or other recognized transfer mechanisms. Messages you send us may be processed in Montenegro or wherever our email provider operates.

9. Retention

Information on your device stays until you delete the App or its data. Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request and for our records, and are then deleted or archived. Third-party services keep information according to their own policies.

10. Your Rights

Most information described in this Policy stays on your device or with the third-party services above, so there is often little for us to access or delete ourselves. Where we hold information about you, such as support correspondence, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete it by writing to wellias.support@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under law, such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For information held by Apple, RevenueCat, or Google, you can exercise your rights with them directly. We aim to respect these rights for all users regardless of location.

11. Age Requirement

WELLIAS is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone below that age. If you believe we have received such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information we hold. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Governing Law

This Policy is governed by the laws of Montenegro, except where mandatory data-protection or consumer-protection law of your country of residence gives you rights that cannot be waived. If this Policy is provided in several languages and they differ, the English version prevails, except where mandatory law of your country provides otherwise.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time, for example when we change an advertising or purchase provider or add features. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest change. For material changes we will give notice in the App or on the App Store listing where reasonably possible.

15. Contact Us

Questions or requests about this Policy: wellias.support@gmail.com
